# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue with the code and the data, not with the
person.

fixitalia deals with public spending and parliamentary records, which are
political subjects. Disagreement about what the numbers mean is welcome and is
part of the point. Contempt for the people discussing them is not.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by mistakes, and learn
  from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its readers.
- Remember that most contributors here are not native English speakers, and
  that the project itself is Italian. Read past imperfect phrasing rather than
  correcting it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using the project's data to harass, threaten, or target a named individual.
  The data describes people acting in public office; it is published here to
  inform, not to build a case against anyone.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, privately, through the contact
channel listed on the site's Contatti page. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this
policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour
they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
